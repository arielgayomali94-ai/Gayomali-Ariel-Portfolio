Gayomali's Frog Legs Adobo "Kokak" Recipe

Ingredinets

  • 500-750g frog legs, cleaned and cut into serving pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 3-4 tbsp venigar (cane venigar)
  • 1 head garlic, crushed and minced
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger, crushed and sliced
  • 1-2 tsp whole black peppercorns
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • cooking oil
  • optional: sugar or chill for added flavor

Procedures

  1. Saute garlic, onion, and ginger in oil until fragrant.
  2. Brown the frog legs, then add soy sauce, peppercorns, and bay leaves.
  3. Simmer with water until the meat is tender.
  4. Add venigar and simmer for 2-3 minutes
  5. Season with salt or soy sauce to taste.
  6. Serve hot with rice

Essential Equipments

  1. Wok or Large Skillet/Frying Pan
  2. Cooking Pot or Casserole
  3. Spatula
  4. Kitchen Knife and Cutting Board
  5. Tongs
  6. Stariner/Colander
